Privacy information

SmartNow Data Venture uses information you choose to submit to respond to enquiries, support requests, bug reports and improvement suggestions.

Website analytics

Analytics are optional. If you choose Allow analytics, the website stores a first-party pseudonymous visitor identifier for up to six months and records page views, page paths, referring website host, broad device type and browser family. A keyed hash of the connecting IP address is used for abuse prevention and repeat-visit analysis; the full IP address is not stored in the analytics table. The tracker does not collect names, email addresses, exact location, age or gender.

If you decline, the analytics tracker does not record page views or create its analytics identifier. Clear this site's cookie and browser storage to reset your choice; the consent banner will then appear again.

Forms and support requests

When you submit a form, SmartNow stores the name, email address, request category, subject and details you provide so the team can handle and respond to the request. Do not submit passwords, payment-card details or other sensitive information in the form.

Contact

For questions about a submission or this notice, use the contact and feedback form.

This notice describes the website features currently implemented. It is not a substitute for a formal legal review of the organisation's data-protection obligations.
