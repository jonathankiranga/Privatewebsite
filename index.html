Product information

What each solution covers

These summaries describe verified areas in the available code and product documentation. They are not a guarantee that every feature is enabled in every deployment.

School Management Suite

Role-based school workflows

Available codebases include teacher workflows for attendance, examinations, report cards and lesson plans; school leadership workflows for fees, attendance, student records and class management; and parent-facing academic reports and fee statements. The related portals and backend must be assessed together for a complete deployment.

Smart ERP

Operational control and approvals

The application dashboard includes purchase-order approvals, store requests, payment-voucher approvals, price-list approvals, laboratory production entries, stock replenishment monitoring, bank balances, tasks and activities. The complete module and deployment scope should be confirmed in a walkthrough.

Smart LIMS

Laboratory information management

Documented workflows include sample registration, departmental receipt, chemical and microbiological results, result review and approval, subcontractor allocation, chain of custody, sample disposal, laboratory reports and certificates of assessment. Some documented menu functions are unavailable in the referenced release.

Smart POS

Sales and inventory records

Reviewed source confirms sales invoice workflows and inventory maintenance, including stock codes, barcodes, quantities, reorder settings, categories and full/loose unit conversion. The complete checkout workflow and deployment readiness require further verification.

Bulk Messaging

Group-based SMS operations

The Android application documentation describes contact and group management, bulk recipient entry, message composition, queued sending, send history and Africa’s Talking gateway settings. Gateway charges, sender ID approval and customer billing are separate matters to confirm.
