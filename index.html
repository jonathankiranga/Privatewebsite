▤POS App 1 · Supermarket

“How do I move supermarket queues fast without losing stock or cash control?”

Supermarket POS pairs auto-focused barcode scanning (F2), aisle quick-pick tiles, and additive KES cash tendering (Exact, 50–1,000) with real-time stock control.

  • Duplicate-barcode quantity increment & Hold/Recall
  • M-Pesa reference checks & X/Z shift reports
  • Atomic stockledger & FIFO stockregister deduction
  • Shared-hosting pos_print_jobs thermal printing

✂POS App 2 · Hair Salon

“How do I book appointments, assign stylists, and calculate commissions accurately?”

Hair Salon POS connects client bookings, stylist skill mapping, till checkout, and automatic commission ledgers in one transaction.

  • booked → arrived → in_service → completed flow
  • Locks booked lines against duplicate checkouts
  • Percentage (VAT-excluded) or fixed stylist rules
  • salon_service_commissions payout & audit tracking

◫POS App 3 · Touch Terminal

“How do cashiers run a full till on a touchscreen without a mouse or keyboard?”

Touch POS Terminal delivers a tablet- and counter-ready interface with oversized controls, shift management, and offline-safe receipt printing.

  • 44px–62px touch targets & +/- quantity steppers
  • Sticky category chips & full-screen catalogue
  • Live Open Shift, X-Report & Z-Close controls
  • Local Windows/Node print agent polling over HTTPS

More than a feature list

Less fragmented work. Clearer operational records.

A useful system does more than store information. It helps people complete a process in the right order and retain the records needed for follow-up. Across the SmarterNow range, the focus is on practical steps: capture the transaction or activity, process it through the available checks, and make the resulting record available for review and reporting.
