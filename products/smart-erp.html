Backend Architecture Q&A

How does the shared ERP backend keep every app balanced?

01

“Why is the ERP in the backend instead of a separate app?”

When a cashier scans milk at the supermarket till, a stylist completes a salon booking, or a lab confirms a test invoice, the transaction posts directly into GeneralLedger, BankTransactions, debtorsledger, and CustomerStatement inside the same MySQL transaction—zero manual re-entry.

02

“How does it prevent stock mismatches and unapproved payouts?”

Purchases and goods receipts build FIFO cost layers in stockregister and stockledger. Back-office controls enforce period locking, multi-step voucher approvals (ApproveVoucher.php, ApprovePurchase.php), bank reconciliation, and a full AuditTrail.

03

“Can I manage Chart of Accounts, Budgets, and VAT in one place?”

Yes. The shared backend includes ChartofAccounts.php, Budgets.php, CashFlowReport.php, BankReconciliation.php, and configurable postinggroups + vatcategory rules used automatically by every front-end app.

04

“Does it support multi-currency and tax-ready posting?”

Yes. Every ledger entry records currency, exchange rate, VAT rate, and VAT account mapping so financial statements and tax summaries stay consistent across all connected apps.
