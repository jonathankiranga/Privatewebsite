The School Management Suite is made up of connected teacher, headteacher and parent applications supported by a school API. What School Management Suite does

A closer look at the functional areas present in the reviewed application.

Teacher portal: daily classroom work

Teachers can mark and track student attendance by class and date, record CAT and examination scores, prepare lesson plans, view class performance reports and enter competency ratings. The portal includes a mobile-friendly interface, install prompt, push-notification support and local attendance storage/synchronisation components.

School leadership: staff and class administration

Headteachers can manage teacher and bursar records, activate or deactivate staff, assign teachers to classes, manage class lists and oversee student records. School leadership screens include dashboards, class management, student lists and year-end/promotion workflows.

Fees and school finance administration

Create fee items by academic year and term, mark fees as optional and assign fee items to classes. The wider school environment includes fee management and payment-related administration, including a bursar role that uses the Bazar Pay portal.

Attendance and academic performance oversight

Review attendance analytics, class reports, CAT and examination records, competency ratings, strand performance and level distribution. These views help school leaders monitor both participation and academic progress.

Parent portal: child information

Parents sign in using a phone number or email verification code (OTP), then access linked children, school information, academic reports and fee statements. The portal supports selecting the academic year and term for relevant records.

Parent payments and premium access

The parent application includes a premium-access renewal workflow with M-Pesa checkout initiation and payment-status checking. The availability and price of parent premium access should be confirmed for the current school deployment.

School marketplace

The parent application includes a searchable school market with categories such as uniforms, textbooks, stationery, transport, tuition and food. Listings display seller details and contact options where provided.

School-to-parent communication

The headteacher interface includes a broadcast workflow for sending WhatsApp messages to premium parents in the school. Delivery depends on the configured messaging service and the recipient eligibility rules.

How the workflow fits together

A typical school workflow starts with school leadership setting up staff, classes and fee items. Teachers then record attendance, assessments, lesson plans and competency ratings. Parents use the OTP-protected portal to review academic information and fee statements for their linked children.

Implementation and availability

The repositories contain separate frontend applications and API integrations. Feature availability depends on the deployed backend, school configuration, role permissions, notification services and payment setup. Do not assume that every portal or payment flow is active merely because its frontend code exists.
