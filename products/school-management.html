SmartNow solution

School Management Suite

School operations across the roles that keep a school moving.

The reviewed codebase contains separate teacher, school leadership, parent and payment-related interfaces, supported by a school management API. The portals are components of one school-management offering, not five unrelated products.

Verified capability areas

Teacher workflows

The teacher portal includes attendance, examinations, report cards, lesson plans, class reports and competency ratings.

School leadership

The headteacher portal includes school dashboards, teacher management, fees, attendance analytics, examinations, student records, class management and promotions.

Parent access

The parent portal includes academic reports and fee statements, with a phone/OTP login flow present in the reviewed source.

Payments and administration

Related interfaces cover payment recording, student balances, payment history, account statements, school records and subscription-related administration.

Availability and scope

The source repositories establish these interface and API areas, but do not by themselves prove that every end-to-end flow is deployed, integrated or available to every school.
