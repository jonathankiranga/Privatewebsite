What Smart POS does

A closer look at the functional areas present in the reviewed application.

Barcode-first product entry

The checkout screen focuses the barcode/search field on load. Cashiers can scan a product, press Enter or use the add action, and add the matched item to the sale. Product lookup and search are also available through the POS scripts.

Sales basket and quantity control

Build a sale from multiple product lines, change quantities, remove selected lines and recalculate line totals and the sale total. The checkout includes keyboard shortcuts for common cashier actions.

Cash tender and change

Enter cash received or use quick tender buttons. The system calculates whether the tender covers the sale and displays the change due or remaining balance before completing the cash payment.

M-Pesa payment reference verification

Capture the customer's Kenyan M-Pesa phone number and transaction reference. The cashier must validate the phone/reference format and explicitly confirm that payment has been checked against the customer's confirmation message or statement before proceeding. This is a cashier verification workflow, not an automatic STK push confirmation claim.

VAT-aware pricing

Calculate VAT and line totals using the VAT rate and the configured sales posting group's VAT-inclusive setting. This keeps the displayed checkout totals aligned with the configured tax treatment.

Receipts and local printer support

Generate the receipt after the sale is committed. A Windows print agent polls the hosted application for queued receipts, sends jobs to the selected local printer and acknowledges or retries jobs. A printer outage does not reverse an already committed sale.

Inventory and stock configuration

Maintain item descriptions, item codes, barcodes, stock classification, posting groups, categories, units, reorder levels and economic order quantities. Inventory supports full and loose units with a part-per-unit conversion value.

Sales and operational reporting

The wider application includes sales invoices, sales orders, sales returns, daily and monthly sales reports, stock balance and stock value reports, and related finance and inventory records.

How the workflow fits together

Scan or search for an item → add it to the sale → adjust quantities → review VAT and the total → record cash tender or verify the M-Pesa reference → commit the sale → print the receipt through the configured local printer agent.

Implementation and availability

The main branch confirms the barcode checkout, cash and manual M-Pesa-reference verification workflow. The print agent requires installation and printer compatibility checks on the cashier computer. Dedicated salon and touchscreen development lines exist separately; confirm the exact release, terminal layout, devices and business-specific workflows before selecting a deployment.
