Everyday Questions Answered

What happens behind every receipt?

01

“What if the receipt printer is offline when a customer pays?”

Every sale commits to MySQL first inside a single atomic transaction. The receipt is then queued in pos_print_jobs for the local print agent to poll and print—so printer outages never roll back a paid sale.

02

“How do I stop cashiers from reusing M-Pesa codes or closing a till short?”

M-Pesa checkout validates Kenyan 2547XXXXXXXX / 2541XXXXXXXX numbers and rejects duplicate codes against receiptheader.externalref. Every sale requires an open shift in pos_shifts, and Z-Close compares expected vs. counted drawer cash.

03

“Does selling at the till actually deduct stock and update my cashbook?”

Yes. Each receipt writes negative movements to stockledger, consumes oldest-first FIFO layers in stockregister, posts the sales invoice to GeneralLedger and debtorsledger, and debits Cash or M-Pesa in BankTransactions.

04

“How is VAT handled—both inclusive and exclusive?”

VAT behavior is controlled by your ERP posting group (postinggroups.VATinclusive) and item tax mapping (inventorypostinggroup → vatcategory), keeping the basket, receipt, and General Ledger aligned.
