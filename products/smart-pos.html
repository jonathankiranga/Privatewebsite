Smart POS

Sales invoicing and inventory records for business operations.

The reviewed source confirms sales invoice workflows and inventory maintenance. This page does not claim that every conventional checkout or payment-terminal workflow has been verified.

Verified capability areas

Sales invoicing

The sales invoice workflow works with released sales orders and records invoice-related customer, salesperson, currency, document and batch information.

Inventory records

Stock maintenance includes item codes, barcodes, descriptions, stock types, posting groups, quantities and categories.

Stock control

Reorder levels and economic order quantity fields are present in the inventory source.

Unit configuration

Inventory records support full and loose units with a part-per-unit conversion value.

POS solutions for different businesses

Salon POS

A point-of-sale direction for hair salons and beauty businesses. The source repository contains a dedicated hair-salon POS development branch; confirm the enabled salon workflows in the release being offered.

Touchscreen POS

A touch-oriented POS interface for counter and kiosk use. A dedicated touchscreen terminal development branch exists; confirm supported devices, checkout functions and printer integration for the deployment.

Small Supermarket POS

For small retail and supermarket operations requiring sales and stock control. The reviewed source confirms sales invoicing and inventory records, but the complete barcode-scanning checkout, receipt printing and payment-terminal workflow must be verified before purchase.
